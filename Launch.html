Where the Roses Remember • Chapter I

The Unsealed Envelope

Rain tapped a slow, familiar cadence against the arched parlor windows of West Eleventh Street. Clara knelt beside the lifted heart-pine floorboards, her fingers brushing the edge of a cedar box that smelled faintly of dried rosewater and winter coal.

Inside lay seven letters tied together with a frayed ribbon of deep burgundy silk. None bore a stamp, yet the topmost envelope was addressed in a sweeping, unhurried hand: For whoever stays long enough to listen.

“Some stories are written on the wind,” the first line read, “but ours was always meant to be pressed between the pages.”
