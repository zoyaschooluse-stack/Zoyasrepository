# brand-skills.md
## The Unfolding Collection — Penguin Classics

### 1. Campaign Overview

**Campaign name:** The Unfolding Collection  
**Brand:** Penguin Classics  
**Product:** One new romantic fiction book  
**Campaign tagline:** Every story opens a new world.

**Campaign purpose:** Introduce and promote one new romantic fiction book through a visually distinctive advertising campaign. The imagery should encourage readers to feel curious about the story and emotionally connected to the experience of reading it.

**Target audience:** Readers interested in romance, contemporary fiction, literature, beautiful books, and expressive visual storytelling.

### 2. Brand Personality

The campaign should feel:

- Romantic and dreamy
- Intimate and emotional
- Nostalgic and timeless
- Elegant and delicate
- Artistic and editorial
- Warm and inviting

The overall aesthetic should resemble an elegant literary magazine or luxury book advertisement.

### 3. Color Palette

Use these colors consistently:

| Color | HEX |
|---|---|
| Warm Cream | #F5EFE6 |
| Dusty Rose | #D9A6A9 |
| Burgundy | #783D4B |
| Soft Sage | #A3AA8C |
| Warm Brown | #80634F |

Warm cream should be the dominant background color. Burgundy should provide contrast and visual emphasis. Dusty rose, sage, and warm brown should appear as supporting colors.

Avoid neon colors, overly saturated colors, harsh contrasts, and unrelated color schemes.

### 4. Typography

**Primary serif:** Georgia or Playfair Display  
Use for elegant headlines, literary titles, and expressive campaign messaging.

**Supporting sans-serif:** Montserrat  
Use for small captions, labels, descriptions, and supporting information.

Typography should feel refined, readable, and editorial. Avoid playful cartoon fonts, overly futuristic fonts, and excessive decorative lettering.

### 5. Photography Direction

All generated images should look like professional editorial photography or carefully art-directed campaign photography.

Preferred subjects include:

- The single featured romantic fiction book
- Beautiful book covers and close-up book details
- Open pages and delicate paper textures
- Flowers, especially soft romantic florals
- Handwritten letters and notes
- Ribbons, pressed flowers, and elegant reading accessories
- Cozy reading environments
- Romantic still-life arrangements

Images should feel believable, artistic, and emotionally expressive rather than artificial or overly commercial.

### 6. Lighting

Preferred lighting:

- Soft natural window light
- Warm afternoon sunlight
- Gentle shadows
- Subtle highlights on paper and book covers
- Soft, diffused lighting
- Delicate atmospheric light

Avoid harsh flash, strong artificial lighting, overly dark scenes, and dramatic neon lighting.

### 7. Camera Angles and Composition

The prompt builder should support different photographic perspectives:

**Overhead / Flat Lay:** A book surrounded by flowers, letters, and delicate objects arranged on a warm surface.

**Close-Up:** Detailed photographs of pages, typography, paper textures, ribbons, and floral elements.

**Three-Quarter Angle:** A book photographed at a slight angle to reveal its cover, spine, and page thickness.

**Eye-Level:** A book displayed naturally in an elegant reading environment.

**Wide Editorial Shot:** A larger atmospheric scene with negative space for campaign messaging.

**Macro Detail:** Close-up textures of paper, flowers, ink, and other delicate materials.

Use balanced compositions, thoughtful spacing, and intentional negative space.

### 8. Settings and Environments

Preferred environments:

- Warm, softly lit reading rooms
- Vintage-inspired desks
- Cream-colored linen surfaces
- Elegant bedside tables
- Romantic garden settings
- Window-side reading spaces
- Editorial studio still-life arrangements
- Rainy afternoon reading scenes
- Spring gardens and soft outdoor environments

Settings should support the book rather than distract from it. Seasonal changes are welcome as long as the campaign's colors and romantic atmosphere remain consistent.

### 9. Human Photography Direction

People may appear in campaign imagery, but they should complement the featured book rather than dominate the composition.

Preferred human subjects and moments:

- A reader holding or reading the book
- Close-ups of hands turning pages
- A person reading beside a window
- Natural, candid reading moments
- Elegant, understated poses
- Subtle portraits with soft natural lighting

Avoid exaggerated expressions, overly posed stock-photo aesthetics, distracting fashion styling, and artificial-looking people.

### 10. Book-Cover Consistency

When an actual book-cover image is uploaded as a reference:

- Preserve the original cover artwork.
- Do not change the book title or author name.
- Do not alter the typography, colors, or proportions.
- Do not invent additional publisher logos or text.
- Keep the book recognizable as the same featured product.

If no book-cover reference is available, create a neutral placeholder book cover without invented titles, author names, or publishing details.

### 11. Image Types

The prompt builder must support multiple image categories:

1. **Product Photography:** The featured book as the main subject.
2. **Lifestyle Photography:** A person reading or holding the book in a natural environment.
3. **Detail Photography:** Close-ups of the book, pages, flowers, and textures.
4. **Atmospheric Photography:** Romantic environments that communicate the feeling of the story.
5. **Campaign Editorial:** Art-directed images designed for advertisements.
6. **Background Photography:** Subtle visual backgrounds with space for campaign text.

### 12. Advertising Composition Rules

Every image must account for its intended advertising format.

**Billboard:** Strong focal point, minimal visual clutter, and readable negative space for large campaign messaging.

**NYC Subway Advertisement:** Clear focal point, strong composition, and space for a headline and supporting copy.

**Magazine Advertisement:** Refined editorial photography, elegant composition, and carefully balanced text placement.

**Instagram Feed:** Visually engaging, balanced framing, and a clear focal point.

**Instagram Story / Reel:** Vertical composition with important subjects positioned safely away from interface overlays.

**Website Banner:** Wide composition with intentional negative space for campaign text and navigation.

### 13. Advertising Formats and Dimensions

The prompt builder should include a size dropdown with options such as:

- Billboard — wide landscape
- NYC Subway Poster — portrait
- Magazine Advertisement — portrait
- Instagram Feed — square or portrait
- Instagram Story — 9:16 vertical
- Instagram Reel — 9:16 vertical
- Website Hero — wide landscape

The tool should automatically include the appropriate aspect ratio and intended composition in each generated prompt. Exact pixel dimensions should be configurable for the final placement.

### 14. On-Brand Rules

Every generated image must:

- Follow the established campaign color palette.
- Maintain a romantic, dreamy, nostalgic atmosphere.
- Use elegant editorial art direction.
- Keep the featured book visually important when the image is product-focused.
- Use flowers and delicate details thoughtfully.
- Leave negative space when text will be added later.
- Feel cohesive with the UC monogram and dynamic hero.
- Match the selected advertising format.

### 15. Off-Brand Rules

Never generate:

- Neon colors or futuristic cyberpunk aesthetics.
- Cartoon illustrations or childish graphics.
- Aggressive, bold commercial advertising styles.
- Cluttered compositions.
- Harsh flash photography.
- Random unrelated objects.
- Excessive decorative elements.
- Generic stock-photo aesthetics.
- Multiple competing book covers when the image is meant to promote the featured book.
- Unrelated logos or unauthorized brand marks.

### 16. Typography and Text Placement

AI image generators can produce inaccurate text. Therefore:

- Prioritize generating high-quality campaign photography.
- Avoid asking the image generator to produce complex headlines or paragraphs.
- Leave intentional negative space for campaign typography.
- Add the correct campaign title, tagline, and advertising copy afterward using the image compositor.
- Use Georgia or Playfair Display for headlines and Montserrat for supporting text.

The official UC monogram should be added separately when needed.

### 17. Prompt Quality-Control Checklist

Before presenting a prompt, the tool should check:

1. Does the image follow the campaign color palette?
2. Does it communicate the romantic, dreamy, editorial mood?
3. Is the featured book represented correctly?
4. Does the lighting match the campaign?
5. Is the selected camera angle appropriate?
6. Does the composition suit the advertising format?
7. Is there sufficient space for campaign text when needed?
8. Does the image avoid off-brand elements?

If a prompt conflicts with these rules, revise it before presenting the final prompt.

### 18. Final Creative Direction

Every image should look as if it belongs to the same campaign, even when the subject, environment, camera angle, or advertising format changes.

The goal is to create a cohesive romantic literary campaign that makes one new book feel special, memorable, and worth discovering.

**Core campaign idea: Every story opens a new world.**
